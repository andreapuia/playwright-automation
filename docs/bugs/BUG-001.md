**User:** `problem_user`
**Severity:** High
**Priority:** High

**Steps to reproduce:**
1. Log in with `problem_user` / `secret_sauce`
2. Click **Add to cart** on the three most expensive products

**Observation:** the user cannot buy the desired products. The main business flow is blocked.
