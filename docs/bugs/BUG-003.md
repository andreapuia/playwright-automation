# BUG-003: Inventory price differs from cart price
 
**User:** `visual_user`
**Severity:** High
**Priority:** High
 
**Steps to reproduce:**
1. Log in with `visual_user` / `secret_sauce`
2. Note the product prices on the inventory page
3. Add the products to the cart
4. Open the cart and compare the prices

**Actual result:** at least one product has a different price in the inventory than in the cart (observed example: $79.90 in the inventory, $15.99 in the cart). The incorrect value may differ between runs.
 
**Expected result:** each product's price in the cart is identical to the one shown in the inventory.
 
**Observation:** the user sees inconsistent prices. In addition, selecting products by price relies on incorrect data, so the products chosen as "most expensive" may be wrong.

**Also seen in Scenario 2:** the displayed prices are random and change on every page load, so after sorting by price the list does not appear sorted (e.g. `95.89, 62.65, 86.81, 93.02, 39.75, 28.35` for "Price (low to high)"). The sorting itself likely uses the real prices.
